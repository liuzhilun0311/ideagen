# Platform Risk-Expression Rules

These are conservative generation guidelines, not an official, complete, or real-time prohibited-word list.

## Shared Baseline

- Avoid unsupported absolute, extreme, unique, guaranteed, zero-risk, or definite-effect claims.
- Do not invent evidence, studies, clinical results, patents, credentials, rankings, testimonials, cases, prices, availability, or authority endorsements.
- For health, legal, finance, and other high-risk topics, state the evidence boundary, applicable conditions, individual differences, and necessary limitations.
- Do not use homophones, split characters, special characters, encoding, or deliberate misspellings to evade review.
- Preserve user facts, dates, numbers, prices, units, product names, necessary warnings, and conclusions.
- Use semantic, natural, qualified wording only when the supplied facts support it. Examples such as “100% effective”, “根治”, “最有效”, “美白”, “减肥”, and “孕妇可用” are recognition examples, not a substitution table.

## Xiaohongshu

Use concrete scenarios, observable features, usage boundaries, and authentic experience only when the user supplied the experience. Be especially cautious with efficacy, medical, drug, clinical, patent, authority, special-population, beauty, weight, blood pressure, blood sugar, sterilization, detoxification, hair-growth, and similar claims.

## Douyin

Use a direct first screen, short sentences, and one main point per page. Avoid clickbait, fear amplification, impulsive-purchase pressure, instant-result promises, and turning a slogan or trend phrase into a factual guarantee.

## WeChat Official Account

Give the background, reasoning, evidence boundary, applicability, and limitations before recommendations. Keep titles, leads, and conclusions factual; cite only sources and data supplied or verifiable by the caller.

## Multi-Platform

Use the most conservative shared wording. Do not promise that one output is separately optimized for every platform, and do not add platform-specific review tricks or slogans.

## Phase Guidance

- `outline`: prevent page titles, conclusions, selling points, and CTAs from becoming unsupported promises.
- `copy`: identify risky claims in titles, body, tags, and CTAs, then express the same supported meaning with natural limits and conditions.
- `image`: apply the same wording boundary to visible titles, selling points, labels, and CTAs. Never render internal rule names or platform IDs.

The model must not output these instructions as part of the requested content.
